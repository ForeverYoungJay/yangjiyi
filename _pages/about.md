<div class="en" markdown="1">

I am a PhD student in Materials Science and Engineering at the **Institute of Science Tokyo** (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology) and a **NIMS Junior Researcher** in the Materials Modeling Group, supervised by Prof. Masahiko Demura and Prof. Yoshinao Kobayashi.

My research sits at the intersection of **AI for Science** and **computational materials science**:

- **LLM multi-agent systems** that automate crystal plasticity simulation workflows (CrystalPlasticitySim, DAMASK-MCP, DAMASK Copilot)
- **Literature mining** of crystal plasticity constitutive parameters with LLMs (CPExtractor)
- **Graph neural networks** for polycrystalline stress prediction conditioned on single-crystal responses (SCR-GNO)
- **Crystal plasticity modeling** with DAMASK, e.g. anisotropic cold-rolling of Ni₃Al single crystals

In 2026 I was an exchange student at **RWTH Aachen University** and a visiting researcher in Prof. Dierk Raabe's group at **MPI for Sustainable Materials (MPI-SusMat)**, Germany.

<div class="zh" markdown="1">

我是**东京科学大学**（原东京工业大学）材料科学与工程专业博士研究生，同时担任**日本物质·材料研究机构（NIMS）**材料建模组初级研究员，导师为出村雅彦教授、小林能直教授。

我的研究方向是 **AI for Science** 与**计算材料学**的交叉：

- **大语言模型多智能体系统**：自动化晶体塑性模拟流程（CrystalPlasticitySim、DAMASK-MCP、DAMASK Copilot）
- **文献参数抽取**：利用大语言模型从科学文献中提取晶体塑性本构参数（CPExtractor）
- **图神经网络**：基于单晶响应的多晶应力预测与小样本迁移（SCR-GNO）
- **晶体塑性模拟**：基于 DAMASK 的数值模拟，如 Ni₃Al 单晶冷轧各向异性研究

2026 年，我作为交换生赴德国**亚琛工业大学（RWTH Aachen）**，并在**马克斯·普朗克可持续材料研究所（MPI-SusMat）** Dierk Raabe 教授团队进行访问研究。
